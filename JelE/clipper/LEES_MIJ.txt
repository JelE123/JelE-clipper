JelE Clipper
============

What it does
- While you stream and record, it remembers your clip markers and what happens in chat.
- After your stream it finds the best moments by itself: where you pressed Clip, where chat went off,
  where it suddenly got loud, and your multikills, aces and clutches.
- It makes vertical shorts (9:16) from them: your cam on top, your game below, with captions, zooms and a title.
  The best ones are waiting in your inbox. You only choose: keep or remove.

Install once
1. Double-click install.bat and wait until it says "Done!".
2. Start the Clipper with start.bat and go through the setup wizard (Settings > Setup wizard).

Every stream
1. Start start.bat (or let OBS start it with the script jele_clipper.lua). Leave the black window open.
2. Record in OBS while you stream. Tip: Settings > Output > Recording format MKV, and
   Settings > Advanced > "Automatically record when streaming".
3. Good moment? Press Ctrl+Alt+K, "Clip!" in your JelE panel, or let a mod type !clip.
4. Stop recording. A few minutes later your shorts are in your inbox.

Your shorts are in the "shorts" folder next to this file.

CAPTIONS WITHOUT VOICELINES AND MUSIC
The captions should only pick up your voice. That works best with your mic on its own audio track:
1. OBS > Edit > Advanced Audio Properties: tick Track 3 for your Mic (and leave 1 on).
   Desktop Audio and music NOT on Track 3.
2. Settings > Output > Output Mode: Advanced > Recording tab > Audio Track: tick 1 and 3.
3. Settings > Output > Audio tab > Track 3 > Name: Mic
The clipper then finds that track by itself. Under Settings > Which audio you can listen to each track.
Without a separate track the clipper filters out music and the lines in voicelines.txt.

KEYS AND PRIVACY
- Passwords and keys (OBS, HenrikDev, Twitch) go in Settings > Keys. They are only stored on this PC
  (in C:\JelE\mascotte\config.js) and never go into an error report or onto GitHub.
- Your Twitch name, Riot name and team name are there too.

UPDATES
- Settings > Updates. New versions come from GitHub by themselves. If a new version does not start
  properly, the Clipper puts the previous one back. You can go back yourself too:
  Settings > Updates > Restore, or double-click herstel.bat.
- Something odd after an update? Start veilig.bat: then it makes shorts without effects and music.

WHAT IS WHERE
- Inbox: your automatic shorts after every stream. Keep, edit or remove (Y / E / X).
- Streams: all your recordings. Open one to see the moments, or look through the video yourself.
  "Add video" takes a link (Twitch VOD or clip, Medal, YouTube), a file or a whole folder.
- Editor: tabs on the right (Layout, Text, Effects, Audio, Export). Preview shows the result in seconds.
  Shift+click words to cut a part. "Cut silences" removes all pauses. Tab walks through unsure words.
- Shorts: search, tags, favourites, archive and a recycle bin (7 days). The folder icon has the export
  pack and extras: Discord, GIF, stills, meme, 4:5 feed and team folder.
- Insights: what works, based on the views you fill in.
- Settings > Brand: your colour, logo, watermark, font and end screen. Several profiles are possible;
  pick one per short in the editor (Export tab).
- Settings > Live clips: clips during your stream with the OBS replay buffer. Stream Deck: let a button
  open http://localhost:8765/api/clip
- Phone: turn on phone access, scan the QR code and choose "Add to home screen".
- System: self-test, error report, storage, log and the speech model.

Shortcuts: press ? in the app for the overview.
